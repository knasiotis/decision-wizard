Two fixes to the flow editor.

* Tapping a stub chip now takes you to the node at the other end. It used to
  slide the canvas sideways and stop somewhere that was not the node it had
  just highlighted, which was worst when zoomed out.
* Labels on the connecting lines are readable again. A line could be drawn
  straight across another line's label, and two labels close together could
  print on top of each other. Lines are now drawn underneath every label, and
  labels that would collide are spaced apart.
