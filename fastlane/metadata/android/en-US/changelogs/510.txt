Another go at the stub chips, done a different way.

Tapping a chip worked out where to move the canvas by recalculating the node's
position from the graph layout, the zoom and the screen density. That calculation
was right on paper and wrong on a device, and three releases of correcting it did
not help.

It now measures where the bubble actually is on screen and moves by the
difference. There is no zoom or density in that sum at all, so there is nothing
left to get wrong: whatever the node's distance from the middle turns out to be,
that is how far the canvas moves.
