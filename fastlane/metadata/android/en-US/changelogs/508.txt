Editor and chat fixes.

* Tapping a stub chip now pins the node at the other end to the middle of the
  screen at the zoom you were already using. It previously slid the canvas
  sideways and stopped short of the node, sometimes pushing the graph off the
  screen altogether.
* The branches leaving a question are drawn apart from each other. They used to
  leave from the same point and run along the same line, which read as one line
  that forks rather than as the separate paths they are.
* A back arrow in the chat's top bar. The bottom bar is hidden while a chat is
  open, so the system back gesture used to be the only way out.
