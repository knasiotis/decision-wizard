Editor fixes, and a new setting.

* Answer labels are no longer hidden behind question bubbles. They now sit in the
  clear band between two rows, which nothing else occupies, instead of wherever
  the line happened to be halfway along.
* Lines carry arrowheads, so which way a branch runs is readable — including on
  the long horizontal stretches, which gained a marker of their own.
* New setting, under Settings > Graphs: choose whether a link reaching two
  questions ahead is drawn as a line, or shown as a pair of chips like the longer
  ones already are. Lines threading between other questions can be hard to
  follow; chips are always legible.
* The back button leaves the editor again. After tapping a chip it used to rewind
  the jump instead, which read as undoing an edit. The way back from a jump is
  the chip waiting on the node you landed on.
